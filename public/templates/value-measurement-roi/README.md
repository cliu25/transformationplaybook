# Value Measurement & ROI Templates

This folder contains professional, fillable templates for measuring and demonstrating the value of your AI transformation initiatives. These templates are organized by transformation phase and designed to be practical, easy-to-use tools that domain leads can download and customize.

## 📋 Template Overview

### **ANALYZE PHASE** (3 Templates)

Use these templates during the initial planning and business case development phase:

1. **[Business Case & ROI Template](./business-case-roi-template.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Build a comprehensive business case with ROI projections
   - **When to Use:** Before starting any AI initiative to secure funding and alignment
   - **Key Sections:** Executive summary, problem statement, baseline/target metrics, ROI calculations, resource requirements, risk assessment
   - **Outputs:** Approved business case with finance-validated ROI projections

2. **[Baseline Metrics Tracker](./baseline-metrics-tracker.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Document current-state metrics that will be improved
   - **When to Use:** During business case development, before pilot launch
   - **Key Sections:** Metric definitions, baseline values, data sources, finance validation
   - **Outputs:** Finance-validated baseline metrics for ROI tracking

3. **[Value Hypothesis Canvas](./value-hypothesis-canvas.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Articulate and validate expected value before significant investment
   - **When to Use:** Early in the Analyze phase to align stakeholders
   - **Key Sections:** Problem definition, target users, pain points, proposed solution, success metrics, assumptions to validate
   - **Outputs:** Aligned value hypothesis with clear success criteria

---

### **BUILD PHASE** (3 Templates)

Use these templates during pilot development and testing:

4. **[Pilot Metrics Dashboard Template](./pilot-metrics-dashboard.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Track key metrics weekly during the pilot phase
   - **When to Use:** Throughout the pilot period (typically 4-12 weeks)
   - **Key Sections:** Weekly metric tracking, adoption metrics, technical performance, risk/issue log
   - **Outputs:** Weekly pilot status reports with trend analysis

5. **[Value Evidence Report](./value-evidence-report.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Document pilot results and make go/no-go scaling decision
   - **When to Use:** At the end of the pilot phase
   - **Key Sections:** Pilot overview, metrics performance, value achieved, user feedback, technical performance, lessons learned, recommendations
   - **Outputs:** Comprehensive pilot results report with scaling recommendation

6. **[Instrumentation Checklist](./instrumentation-checklist.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Ensure proper measurement infrastructure is in place
   - **When to Use:** During BUILD phase before pilot launch
   - **Key Sections:** Telemetry requirements, usage tracking, business metrics, EBM/Apptio integration, data automation, dashboards, privacy/compliance
   - **Outputs:** Validated instrumentation readiness for value measurement

---

### **SUSTAIN PHASE** (3 Templates)

Use these templates during production rollout and ongoing operations:

7. **[Monthly Value Report](./monthly-value-report.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Track ongoing value realization and adoption
   - **When to Use:** Monthly after production launch
   - **Key Sections:** Adoption metrics, business outcomes, value realized, user satisfaction, technical performance, optimization opportunities, scaling plans
   - **Outputs:** Monthly executive report on value and adoption

8. **[Value Realization Tracker](./value-realization-tracker.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Provide month-by-month view of cumulative value
   - **When to Use:** Continuously updated throughout production
   - **Key Sections:** Monthly tracking tables, cumulative performance, value breakdown by category, ROI analysis, trends
   - **Outputs:** Long-term value tracking with trend analysis

9. **[Scaling Readiness Assessment](./scaling-readiness-assessment.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Evaluate readiness to scale beyond pilot or initial deployment
   - **When to Use:** Before major scaling decisions
   - **Key Sections:** Value proof points, adoption/satisfaction, technical stability, support model, change management, scaling target, go/no-go decision
   - **Outputs:** Comprehensive readiness assessment with scaling recommendation

## 🎯 How to Use These Templates

### Step 1: Choose the Right Template
- Identify which phase of transformation you're in (Analyze, Build, or Sustain)
- Select the template that matches your current need
- Review the template's "Purpose" and "When to Use" sections to confirm fit

### Step 2: Download the Right Format

**For Excel/Spreadsheet Templates:**
1. Download the `.csv` file (baseline-metrics-tracker, pilot-metrics-dashboard, monthly-value-report, or value-realization-tracker)
2. Open in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets
3. The file will open with all tables and structure preserved
4. Search for `[FILL IN]` markers and replace with your information
5. Save as `.xlsx` for full Excel functionality

**For Word/Document Templates:**
1. Download the `.md` (markdown) file
2. **Option A:** Open in a markdown editor and export to Word/PDF
3. **Option B:** Copy content and paste into Microsoft Word, then format as needed
4. **Option C:** Use an online converter like [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) or [Dillinger](https://dillinger.io/) to convert to .docx
5. Search for `[FILL IN]` markers and replace with your information

### Step 3: Customize and Fill Out
- Replace all `[FILL IN]` markers with your specific information
- Follow instructions in *italics* for guidance on each section
- Delete placeholder rows and add more as needed
- Adjust formatting to match your organization's standards

### Step 4: Collaborate and Validate
- Share with key stakeholders (Finance, EBM, domain leads, executive sponsors)
- Get required validations and sign-offs
- Use the templates as living documents that evolve with your project

### Step 5: Link Templates Together
- Reference previous templates in later phases
- Maintain consistency in metrics and definitions across templates
- Build a complete value story from business case through scaling

## 💡 Best Practices

### For All Templates:
- **Be Specific:** Use concrete numbers, dates, and names instead of vague descriptions
- **Get Finance Validation:** Always validate baseline metrics and value calculations with Finance/EBM
- **Document Assumptions:** Clearly state assumptions so they can be validated or adjusted
- **Update Regularly:** Keep templates current as your project evolves
- **Link to Data:** Reference dashboards, reports, and data sources for transparency

### For Metrics:
- **Define Clearly:** Ensure everyone understands how metrics are calculated
- **Use Consistent Definitions:** Don't change metric definitions mid-project
- **Validate Data Quality:** Ensure data sources are reliable and accurate
- **Track Trends:** Look at trends over time, not just point-in-time snapshots
- **Set Realistic Targets:** Base targets on benchmarks or pilot results, not wishful thinking

### For Value Calculations:
- **Be Conservative:** It's better to under-promise and over-deliver
- **Separate Hard vs. Soft:** Clearly distinguish between hard savings and soft benefits
- **Show Your Work:** Document calculation methods so others can validate
- **Get Finance Buy-in:** Finance must validate and approve value claims
- **Track Actual vs. Projected:** Compare actual results to projections and explain variances

## 🆘 Getting Help

### Common Questions:

**Q: Which template should I start with?**
A: Start with the Value Hypothesis Canvas to align stakeholders, then move to the Business Case & ROI Template.

**Q: Do I need to use all templates?**
A: Use the templates that match your project phase and governance requirements. At minimum, use the Business Case, Pilot Metrics Dashboard, and Monthly Value Report.

**Q: How do I get Finance validation?**
A: Share the Baseline Metrics Tracker and Business Case & ROI Template with your Finance partner early. Schedule a review meeting to walk through calculations and data sources.

**Q: What if my metrics don't improve as expected?**
A: Document the variance in your Monthly Value Report, analyze root causes, and adjust your approach. Transparency is key.

**Q: Can I modify these templates?**
A: Yes! Customize them to fit your organization's needs while maintaining the core structure and validation requirements.
