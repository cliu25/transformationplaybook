## Status Legend

**Trend Indicators:**
- ↑ = Improving
- → = Stable/No Change
- ↓ = Declining

**Status Colors:**
- 🟢 **Green (On Track):** Meeting or exceeding target
- 🟡 **Yellow (At Risk):** 10-20% below target, needs attention
- 🔴 **Red (Off Track):** >20% below target, requires immediate action
